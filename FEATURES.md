# NearU Features

> **NearU: Discover What's Happening Around You.**

NearU is an event discovery, management, and ticketing platform. Built on the open-source Hi.Events foundation, NearU provides tools for organizers to create, manage, sell, and check in attendees for events of all scales.

---

## 1. Events & Discovery

- **Single & Recurring Occurrences**: Schedule one-off gatherings, multi-day conferences, weekly workshops, or recurring nightlife events with granular date, time, and capacity settings.
- **Event Homepage Designer**: Visual designer allowing organizers to customize event landing pages with themes, cover graphics, descriptions, and ticket selections.
- **Lineup & Schedule Management**: Showcase performers, speakers, stages, and session agendas.
- **Organized Multi-Event Hubs**: Dedicated organizer landing pages listing all upcoming and past public events.

---

## 2. Ticketing & Product Management

- **Flexible Ticket Types**: Free admission, paid tickets, tiered pricing (Early Bird, General Admission, VIP), pay-what-you-want donations, and bundled ticket tiers.
- **Add-ons & Merchandise**: Offer merchandise, drink tokens, parking passes, or workshop materials alongside tickets during checkout.
- **Access Control & Secret Codes**: Unlock hidden ticket categories using organizer-defined promotional discount codes.
- **Capacity Management**: Real-time inventory tracking, tier-level caps, shared event-wide limits, and automatic waitlists when tickets sell out.
- **Reserved Seating**: Interactive seat maps with reserved seat selection.

---

## 3. Checkout & Embedded Selling

- **Embeddable Widgets**: Responsive embeddable ticket iframe widget for WordPress, Squarespace, Webflow, Shopify, or custom websites.
- **Custom Attendee Questions**: Collect custom information per ticket holder (dietary restrictions, t-shirt size, emergency contact) with support for text, dropdowns, checkboxes, and required validations.
- **Affiliate & Referral Tracking**: Generate trackable promotional URLs to reward affiliates and track conversion metrics.
- **Analytics & Conversion Pixels**: Native support for Meta Pixel, TikTok Pixel, Google Analytics, and Google Tag Manager.

---

## 4. Payments & Financials

- **Direct Stripe Payouts**: Connect your own Stripe account with direct customer-to-organizer transactions.
- **Multi-Currency Support**: Bill in USD, EUR, GBP, CAD, AUD, and dozens of international currencies.
- **Taxes & Surcharges**: Configure itemized or included tax rates (VAT, sales tax) and optional platform service fees.
- **Offline & Manual Payments**: Record cash, bank transfer, or door sales manually.
- **Refund Workflows**: Issue full or itemized partial ticket refunds directly through Stripe.

---

## 5. Door Management & Check-in

- **Camera & Scanner QR Check-in**: High-speed ticket validation using mobile phone cameras or USB handheld hardware scanners.
- **Synchronized Check-in Lists**: Share live check-in portals with door staff featuring real-time attendee counts.
- **Branded PDF Tickets**: Automated PDF tickets delivered by email featuring personalized QR codes, event information, and instructions.
- **Box Office Door Sales**: In-person ticket sales interface for door staff.

---

## 6. Security & Organization Management

- **Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)**: TOTP authenticator app protection with downloadable recovery codes.
- **Role-Based Access Control**: Assign team members roles with granular permissions (Admin, Editor, Door Staff, Read-Only).
- **Single Sign-On (SSO)**: OIDC and enterprise SSO authentication options.
- **Developer REST API**: RESTful endpoints and API key management with OpenAPI / Scalar reference documentation.
